# Scale Validation & CFA

An introductory presentation on scale validation and confirmatory factor analysis covering data simulation, descriptive statistics, internal consistency, and the specification of measurement models. The slides also address the estimation of ordered categorical variables via the WLSMV estimator, model convergence, fit indices comparison, composite reliability, and latent factor correlations. Worked analyses are generated in R with psych, lavaan, and semTools using a simulated 10-item Likert-type dataset.
